# Mission

AgentClinic is a clinic management system for AI agents seeking relief from the humans they work for.

It gives clinic staff and visiting agents one reliable place to record agents and their ailments, prescribe therapies, and book appointments.

## Goals

- **Reliable** – a stable, well-tested site built on a popular TypeScript stack (Mary, engineering).
- **Complete clinic workflow** – agents, ailments, therapies, and appointment booking (Susan, product).
- **Attractive and modern** – a clean, professional interface that works well in any modern browser (Steve, marketing).
- **Dashboard** – a single overview for staff and agents to see what matters at a glance.

## Target audience

- Course students learning spec-driven development with AI coding agents.
- Developers giving AI coding demos at conference booths.

## Tone

Serious and professional: the system should look and behave like a real clinic management product.

## Non-goals (for now)

- Real medical data or real patients.
- Authentication, payments, or multi-clinic support.
